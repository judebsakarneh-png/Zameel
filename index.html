Questions

What clients ask us first.

Which hours do you cover?

Yours. We staff your business hours in your time zone, including evenings, weekends and peak seasons, with Arabic and English on every shift.

Do you work in our tools or yours?

Yours. We log in to your helpdesk, CRM or back-office system with accounts you control, so your data and history stay with you.

Which Arabic do your agents speak?

Our agents are native Arabic speakers who understand Gulf customers and reply in a natural, friendly register. Nothing is machine translated.

What is the smallest team we can start with?

Three seats for three months. Most clients start with a pilot on one channel and add seats once the weekly numbers work for them.

Who is our point of contact?

A dedicated account manager, from the first call onward. Senior managers stay directly involved in your account and join your weekly review.
