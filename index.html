Trained on your business before they touch a customer.

Every agent goes through a week of training built from your own materials, then has to pass your scorecard before going live. After that, their conversations keep getting reviewed.

  1. Day 1

    Your business

    Products, customers, policies, delivery times, refunds and warranties.

  2. Day 2

    Your tools

    Your helpdesk, order lookup, tagging and saved replies.

  3. Day 3

    Your voice

    Tone of voice, the reply library, and writing standards in Arabic and English.

  4. Day 4

    The hard cases

    Escalation rules, edge cases, data protection and security.

  5. Days 5–6

    Practice

    Mock conversations built from your real past tickets, scored on your scorecard.

  6. Day 7

    Certification

    20 scored mock conversations. An agent who doesn't pass repeats practice and doesn't go live.
