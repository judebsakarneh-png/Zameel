Questions about outsourcing support

What does a customer support outsourcing team handle?

Order tracking, refunds, complaints, pre-sale questions, account issues and anything else in your support queue. We follow your policies and escalate what needs your team.

Will customers know they are talking to an outsourced team?

Not unless you want them to. Agents use your brand name, your tone and your saved replies, inside your own tools.

What is the smallest team we can start with?

Three seats for three months. Most clients start with a pilot on one channel and add seats once the weekly numbers work for them.

How do we keep control of quality?

Every agent is scored against a quality scorecard, and you get a weekly report on volumes, response times and recurring issues. Your account manager joins a weekly review with you.
