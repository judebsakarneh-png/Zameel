Online stores, delivery apps, fintech and insurance teams, travel and subscription businesses: any company whose customers write in Arabic and English and expect a fast answer. Questions about outsourcing support

What does a customer support outsourcing team handle?

Order tracking, refunds, complaints, pre-sale questions, account issues and anything else in your support queue. We follow your policies and escalate what needs your team.

Will customers know they are talking to an outsourced team?

Not unless you want them to. Agents use your brand name, your tone and your saved replies, inside your own tools.

What is the smallest team we can start with?

Three seats for three months. You can start with a pilot on one channel and add seats once the weekly numbers work for you.

How do we keep control of quality?

Every agent is scored against a quality scorecard, and you get a weekly report on volumes, response times and recurring issues. Your account manager joins a weekly review with you.

How long does it take to go live?

Usually two to three weeks from the first call: one week to agree the playbook and set up access, then training on your products and supervised shifts before the team works your queue on its own.

Is customer support outsourcing cheaper than hiring in-house?

For most teams, yes, once you count recruitment, benefits, equipment, supervision and cover for leave. The homepage cost section breaks down what an in-house seat really costs and what is included in a Zameel seat.
