Chat is where customers decide whether to buy again.

On WhatsApp, a slow reply feels like no reply. Customers send voice notes, switch between Arabic and English, and expect the same warmth they would get in a shop.

We staff your chat channels with native Arabic speakers who write naturally in both languages. We work through the WhatsApp Business app or the WhatsApp Business Platform connected to your helpdesk, so every conversation stays in your account and your history.

What is included
  • WhatsApp, Instagram DMs and website live chat
  • First reply in under 2 minutes in covered hours
  • Replies in the language the customer used, never machine translated
  • Your saved replies, tone and escalation rules
  • Works with your helpdesk or the WhatsApp Business app
  • Weekly report on chat volumes, response times and top issues

Who WhatsApp and chat support is for

Brands that sell or serve customers over chat: online stores, restaurants and delivery, clinics and beauty, education, travel and app-based services. If WhatsApp is where your customers ask about orders, bookings and refunds, a slow or monolingual reply costs you repeat business.

We set up quick replies, labels and away messages with you, agree when a chat becomes a ticket or goes to your team, and track every conversation in the same weekly report: chats handled, first reply time, resolution on first contact and the questions that came up most. You can see how the work looks on the example dashboard linked from the homepage.

Questions about WhatsApp support

Do you need access to our WhatsApp number?

We work inside the setup you already have. With the WhatsApp Business Platform connected to a helpdesk, you give us agent logins you control and can remove at any time.

Can you handle voice notes and photos?

Yes. Agents listen to voice notes and review photos the same way your own team would, and escalate anything that needs a decision from you.

Which hours can you cover?

Yours, including evenings, weekends and peak seasons, with Arabic and English on every shift.

Do you use chatbots or real people?

Real people answer your customers. If you already run a bot for simple questions, our agents pick up the conversations it hands over, so customers never get stuck in a loop.

Can you send order updates and broadcasts?

We can send the template messages you have approved in your WhatsApp Business Platform account and follow up on the replies they bring in. Campaign design and approval stay with your team.
