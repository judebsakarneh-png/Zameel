Peak-season cover

Extra support for Ramadan, White Friday and every rush in between.

Zameel agents are trained on your products before peak week starts, cover the evenings and nights when your customers are most active, and scale back when the rush ends.

Peak weeks decide your year. Your queue should not.

During Ramadan, customers shop late into the night. During White Friday, chat volume can jump in a single afternoon. Hiring and training temporary staff for a few weeks is slow and expensive, and quality usually drops.

We plan the season with you in advance. Agents learn your products, offers and policies before the rush, work the same quality scorecard as your core team, and report to the same account manager you deal with all year.

What is included
  • Training finished before peak week
  • Evening and night cover as your hours stretch
  • Arabic and English on every shift
  • Same quality scorecard as your core team
  • Same account manager all year
  • A clear plan for scaling back after the season

Who peak-season cover is for

Online stores, delivery and grocery apps, airlines and travel agents, telecom and subscription brands: any business whose volume jumps around Ramadan and Eid, White Friday, back-to-school, summer travel or a big launch. Some add a seasonal team on top of their own agents; others hand us the night shift for the season.

We agree a forecast with you from last year's volumes, set staffing by hour, and train agents on your offers and return rules before the first busy day. During the season you get the report daily instead of weekly, so you can see waiting times and top issues while there is still time to act.

Questions about peak-season cover

How early should we plan for Ramadan?

Six to eight weeks before is ideal. That leaves time to agree volumes, train agents on your offers and run a short pilot before the rush.

Can we keep the team after the season?

Yes. You can keep one or two seats year-round, so the people who learned your peak stay with you.

Do seasonal agents work in our tools?

Yes, the same as a core team: your helpdesk, your saved replies and accounts you control.

What if volumes are higher than forecast?

We agree a buffer and an escalation plan before the season: extra hours for trained agents first, then additional agents who completed the same training. Your account manager watches the queue daily and calls you before waiting times slip.

Can you cover Eid and late-night Ramadan hours?

Yes. Ramadan shifts are planned around iftar and suhoor, when customers are most active, and Eid cover is agreed in advance with the same team.
